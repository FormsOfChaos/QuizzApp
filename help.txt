=== DYNAMIC EVALUATION SUITE - HELP & FEATURES ===

1. FILE MANAGEMENT
- Select JSON Bank: Choose your question database from the dropdown. Place .json files in the 'Questions Database' folder.
- Regular Mode: Play the standard quiz.
- Hard Mode: Automatically generated quiz focusing on questions you answered incorrectly in the past.
- Redemption Mode: A special mode for questions you consistently struggle with.
- Self Made Mode: Play your custom playlist of manually selected questions.

2. ANSWERING MODES
- Multiple Choice: Standard 4-option quiz.
- Handwritten Input: Type your answer manually. The app will verify your spelling and highlight exact character mistakes.

3. UTILITIES
- View Scores: Track your overall performance, accuracy, and average scores per bank and game mode.
- Open Quiz Builder: A dedicated tool to create, edit, and manage your custom JSON question banks.
- Open Questions: View the entire loaded database. Features Search (Ctrl+F or 🔍 Search) and Zoom (Ctrl + Scroll).

4. IN-GAME FEATURES
- Intensity Selection: Choose to play 30%, 60%, 80%, or 100% of the loaded questions.
- Self Made Playlist: Check the box during a quiz to save a question to your custom Self Made list for later practice.
- Error Highlighting: If you make a mistake, a popup will show you the exact correct answer.
